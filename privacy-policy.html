Privacy Policy

Floating Clipboard Lite

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Badri Bros handles information when you use Floating Clipboard Lite (the "App").

Floating Clipboard Lite is a clipboard management application designed to help users save, organize, view, favorite, and reuse copied text through a floating interface.

Privacy at a glance: Floating Clipboard Lite is designed to keep clipboard history on your device. We do not operate a cloud service for your clipboard history and we do not sell or share your clipboard content with third parties.

2. Information and Device Access

Clipboard Content

When you copy text on your device, the App may access the copied text so that it can add the text to your clipboard history and make it available for later use. Clipboard content may contain personal or sensitive information, so users should avoid copying information they do not want stored in their clipboard history.

Accessibility Service

Floating Clipboard Lite uses Android's Accessibility Service API as part of its clipboard-related functionality. When the user enables the Accessibility Service, the App may receive accessibility events and text information exposed by Android and other applications where necessary to provide the App's clipboard capture functionality.

The Accessibility Service is not used to provide advertising, sell user data, or build a user profile for marketing purposes.

Display Over Other Apps

The App may request Android's "Display over other apps" permission so that it can show its floating clipboard interface above other applications. This permission is used to provide the App's core floating-interface functionality.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed by the App is used only as necessary to provide its core features, including:

  • Capturing copied text for clipboard history.
  • Displaying saved clipboard entries.
  • Allowing users to favorite and manage saved entries.
  • Allowing users to edit or delete saved entries where supported.
  • Helping users reuse or paste saved text.
  • Providing the floating clipboard interface.

4. Data Storage

Clipboard history and related saved information are stored locally on the user's device for the App's functionality.

Badri Bros does not provide a cloud database or online account system for storing the user's clipboard history.

Floating Clipboard Lite does not transmit clipboard history to a Badri Bros server.

5. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' clipboard content.

We do not share clipboard history with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

The App currently does not use an advertising SDK or a cloud service to process clipboard history.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Clipboard entries are retained locally on the device according to the App's functionality and the user's actions.

Users can delete clipboard entries using the deletion features provided by the App. Favorites are managed separately according to the App's functionality.

Uninstalling the App generally removes its local application data according to the Android operating system and the device's configuration. Device-level backup behavior may vary depending on Android and device settings.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to keep locally stored App data protected. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for avoiding the storage of highly sensitive information in clipboard history if they do not want that information retained on their device.

8. Third-Party Services

The current version of Floating Clipboard Lite does not use a cloud storage service or advertising service to process clipboard history.

If future versions introduce third-party services that materially affect how user data is processed, this Privacy Policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated before or when those changes become applicable.

9. Children's Privacy

Floating Clipboard Lite is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through an online account or cloud service.

Because clipboard content may contain personal information, parents and guardians should guide children in using clipboard applications and permissions appropriately.

10. Your Choices

You can choose whether to grant or revoke Android permissions used by Floating Clipboard Lite through your device settings. Revoking a permission may prevent related App features from working.

You can also manage or delete saved clipboard entries using the App's available controls.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, data practices, or applicable requirements change.

The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be changed when this policy is materially updated. Users should review this page periodically for the latest information.
